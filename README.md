# Noble Bodyguards (Bannerlord 1.5.7)

A Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord mod that adds escorts in eligible town and village missions. Escorts follow near their leader and engage the nearest enemy within 12 meters. Arenas, keeps, siege battles, and stealth missions are excluded.

## Escorts

- **Player:** Up to two healthy, non-hero troops from the main party escort the player. In villages, the player's escorts are mounted while the player is riding and infantry otherwise. Cavalry is preferred when mounted, with infantry as a fallback.
- **Mount changes:** When the player mounts or dismounts in a village, the escorts are replaced with mounted or infantry versions. Each replacement keeps its position, facing, watch state, and combat state.
- **Player companions:** Companions who are not in the player's party get up to two healthy, non-hero troops from their own party. Companions in the player's party do not get escorts.
- **Lords:** Lords whose party is part of an army get up to two healthy, non-hero troops from their own party. Their escorts follow the same mount rule as the player, based on the lord's mount state.
- **Notables:** Village and town notables do not receive escorts.
- **Limits:** There is no global cap on escorts. Each leader's escorts are limited by the troops in that leader's party.

## Settings

The mod uses Mod Configuration Menu v5. Bodyguards are enabled by default. Disable **Enable bodyguards** in the Noble Bodyguards settings to stop escorts from being added to subsequent eligible missions.
