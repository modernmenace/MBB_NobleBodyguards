# Noble Bodyguards (Bannerlord 1.5.7)

A Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord mod that selects up to two healthy, non-hero infantry from the main party to escort the player in eligible town and village missions.

The guards follow near the player and engage the nearest enemy within 12 meters. Arenas, keeps, siege battles, and stealth missions are excluded.

## Settings

The mod uses Mod Configuration Menu v5. Bodyguards are enabled by default; disable them in the Noble Bodyguards settings to prevent them from being added to subsequent eligible missions.
